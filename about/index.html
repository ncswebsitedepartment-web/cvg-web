ABOUT CVG INDIA

Engineering solutions that move industries forward.

CVG India is part of CVG, Inc. USA, a global provider of engineered seating, electrical systems, and vehicle and industrial solutions.

We partner with leading OEMs and businesses to develop products that combine ergonomics, safety, durability, reliability and superior operator experience.

With strong capabilities in engineering, product development, manufacturing and customer support, CVG India serves a diverse range of applications across commercial vehicles, construction equipment, off-highway and industrial equipment, agricultural machinery and infrastructure projects.

Our approach goes beyond supplying components. We work closely with customers from concept and design through validation, industrialisation and series production to deliver solutions engineered around the specific requirements of each application.
